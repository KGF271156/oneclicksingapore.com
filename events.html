Throughout the year

National dates worth knowing

Chinese New Year: January or February

Reunion dinners, red packets, the Chinatown light-up and the River Hongbao fair.

Hari Raya Puasa: date varies

The end of Ramadan, with the Geylang Serai bazaar and open houses.

National Day: 9 August

Singapore’s independence day, with the parade, fireworks and the national songs.

Deepavali: October or November

The Festival of Lights, with Little India lit up and bazaars on Serangoon Road.

Singapore Grand Prix: September or October

Formula 1 under the lights at Marina Bay.
